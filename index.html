Our Solution

Smarter Protection. Stronger Businesses.

Our proposed cybersecurity solution uses AI to monitor suspicious behavior, detect potential ransomware threats, provide real-time alerts, and help contain affected devices.

  • Designed with small and medium-sized businesses in mind
  • Focused on early warning and faster response
  • Built around simple, understandable alerts
Key Features

Built to Detect, Alert, and Contain

Three core capabilities we are designing to help businesses respond to ransomware sooner.

Early Threat Detection

Identify suspicious activities that may indicate ransomware attacks.

Real-Time Alerts

Receive timely notifications about potential cybersecurity threats.

Automated Containment

Help limit ransomware spread by isolating affected devices.
